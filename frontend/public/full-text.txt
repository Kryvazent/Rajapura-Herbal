About the Company
Rajapura Herbal Drugs Co. (Pvt) Ltd is a leading Sri Lankan manufacturer and distributor of herbal medical products, registered as a legal limited company under the laws of Sri Lanka.
Founded by Dr. Sumith Rajapura, the company develops traditional herbal treatments from more than a century of family experience, knowledge and Ayurvedic practice.
Sri Lanka's indigenous medicine brings together Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani and Desheeya Chikithsa traditions that have served its people for generations.
